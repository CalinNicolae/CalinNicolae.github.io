About the event

This learning experience examined DeepSeek and it's recent advancements with a focus on practical implications. Dimitri Casier walked attendees through how next‑generation models are redefining performance benchmarks while also improving accessibility, computational efficiency, and responsible development practices.

What was covered

The lecture explored how DeepSeek's approach is reshaping parts of the AI landscape through a focus on compute efficiency and openness. Dimitri covered how optimization is becoming a central pillar of modern AI research. The question is no longer only about what a model can do, but also how efficiently it can do it and who can access it. The discussion also touched on responsible AI development and what transparency actually means in practice for large model releases. A particularly valuable segment was the live demonstration of running a large language model entirely on a local machine.

My takeaway

As someone in cybersecurity, AI developments like this matter to me on two levels. The first is the obvious one: AI capabilities are advancing faster than most organisations can track, and that creates new attack surfaces and new tools for both defenders and attackers. The second is subtler: the conversation around efficiency and accessibility signals that more and more powerful AI is becoming available to more and more actors.

AI capabilities are evolving faster than ever. The conversation is shifting from raw performance to efficiency, accessibility, and responsibility.

My verdict

A very interesting session that kept me engaged with the practical and strategic implications of these constantly evolving tools. Dimitri Casier brought expertise and thoughtful insights, as well as making space for a good discussion after. His speech left us with concrete takeaways both technical and conceptual.
