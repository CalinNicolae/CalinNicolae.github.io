// ABOUT ME

Hi, I'm Călin Mihai Nicolae

I'm in my final year of studying Applied Computer Science at Howest, Bruges, specializing in cybersecurity. My core interest is, of course, cybersecurity, with a strong foundation in secure software development. Most recently, I completed an internship at Eniris as my final hands-on experience before graduation.

What drives me

  • Cybersecurity & systems thinking — I'm into network security, penetration testing, and the puzzle of breaking and defending systems. I like understanding attack paths from both sides: how an intruder thinks and how a defender builds resilience.
  • Learning by doing — CTFs, hackathons, meetups, labs and projects. Reading gives me a starting point, but real understanding comes from failing repeatedly and still figuring it out in the end.
  • The bigger picture — AI and new tech are exciting, but they're tools, not silver bullets. They should complement the fundamentals, not replace them. A strong foundation matters now more than ever.
  • Away from keyboard — When I'm not coding or hunting vulnerabilities, I like to play video games, sports, train, play music and reconnect with nature.

What this space is for

This portfolio tracks my journey from September 2023 when I started my studies until now. It contains event reflections, hackathons, and more to come. It's how I document growth and show how I think about the field I'm joining.
